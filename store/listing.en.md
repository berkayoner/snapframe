# SnapFrame — listing (English)

**Name:** SnapFrame — Screenshot, Annotate & Beautify

**Summary** (max 132 characters):
Capture, annotate and beautify screenshots — backgrounds, device mockups, blur. Private: everything stays on your device.

**Category:** Tools

**Description:**

Screenshots that look finished.

SnapFrame turns any web page into a polished, shareable image in seconds,
without leaving your browser and without sending anything anywhere.

CAPTURE EXACTLY WHAT YOU NEED
• Visible area, a selected region, a single element, or the full page (scroll & stitch).
• Straight to the clipboard or your Downloads folder if you like — no editor needed.
• Keyboard shortcuts (Alt+Shift+S / A / F), the toolbar button or the right-click menu.
• Smart full-page capture: sticky headers and cookie bars appear once, not on every slice.

ANNOTATE IN SECONDS
• Rectangles, ellipses, arrows, text labels, numbered steps and a highlighter.
• A magnifier for the small details, and a spotlight that dims everything else.
• Blur or pixelate emails, names and anything else private.
• Crop with aspect-ratio presets, multi-select, undo/redo, and shortcuts for every tool.
• Page-aware: shapes snap to real page elements; one click frames a card or button.
• One-click redaction: emails, phone numbers, card numbers, IBANs and API keys are found for you.

ANIMATE IT
• Export a short MP4, WebM or GIF where your annotations appear one by one, with a camera that follows them and a pointer that clicks them into place.
• Turn a full-page capture into a smooth scrolling video inside a browser or device frame.

MAKE IT BEAUTIFUL
• Gradient and solid backgrounds with padding, rounded corners and shadow.
• Browser, laptop, phone and tablet frames that fit your screenshot, never crop it, and update live as you edit.
• Export PNG or JPG at 1× or 2×, or copy straight to the clipboard.

EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE
• A private library of your recent captures: search, reopen, re-edit.
• Import any image by drag & drop or paste and annotate it too.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No uploads. No tracking. SnapFrame makes no network requests,
and your captures never leave your browser.

Available in English and Turkish. Light and dark themes.
